About

I'm Shivangi, a video editor working across Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Filmora, Photoshop and Illustrator. My work spans two lanes: quick-turnaround social and news edits built for retention and readability, and cinematic, colour-graded films built for mood and pacing. I also build motion graphics — both by hand-placing keyframes and by working AI motion tools into the timeline where they speed things up without flattening the look.
